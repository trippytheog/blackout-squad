01 WHO WE ARE

BUILT IN THE
CHAOS.

The battleground doesn't reward luck alone. It rewards the squad that thinks ahead, moves together and performs when everything is on the line.

Blackout Squad is a PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS esports team united by a shared ambition to compete, improve and make every decision count.

From early rotations to final-circle engagements, we believe in disciplined communication, intelligent positioning and trust in the players beside us. Every match is an opportunity to learn. Every fight is a test of execution.

We are here to build something bigger than individual performances, a squad with its own identity, a competitive mindset and the determination to keep pushing forward.
